About this project

EcoSort AI is a web application that classifies waste from photos using computer vision and recommends how to dispose of it.

Limitations

  • It predicts one dominant item per photo and cannot separate several items.
  • It only knows the categories it was trained on. Unknown items may be mislabeled.
  • Glass, metal and plastic can look similar, so accuracy varies with lighting and background.
  • Disposal advice is general and not a substitute for local rules.

Privacy

Uploaded photos and their predictions are stored on the server for usage statistics and are visible only to administrators. Please do not upload images showing people or personal information.
